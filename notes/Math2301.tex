
MATH 2301 - TEST NOTES

1. DETERMINANTS

Find the determinant:

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 3\\
4 & 5
\end{bmatrix}
$$

For a 2x2 matrix:

$$
\det(A)=ad-bc
$$

$$
\det(A)=2(5)-3(4)=-2
$$
